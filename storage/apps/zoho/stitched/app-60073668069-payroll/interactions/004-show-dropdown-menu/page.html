    Simplify payroll accounting with Zoho Books

    Unleash the power of synergy by effortlessly syncing your payroll data with Zoho Books to supercharge your financial processes. Integrate with Zoho Payroll now and experience seamless accounting for all your payroll needs.

    Try Payroll

    Advantages that come with an Integrated System.

    Automated Journal Entries

    Skip manual accounting as each pay run’s expenses, including earnings, taxes, deductions, and contributions, are automatically recorded under the right accounts.

    Match your Payroll Transactions

    Set up the bank account used to pay your employees, choose how payroll expenses are posted, and easily match them with your bank feeds.

    Customisable Chart of Accounts

    Keep your accounting books organized by mapping payroll expenses and liabilities to the right chart of accounts, effortlessly.

    Powerful features that make running payroll a breeze.

    Easy Employee Management

    Add all your employees' information easily and process their salary payments every month.

    Consistent Statutory Compliance

    Stay compliant with the Government mandated schemes such as EPF, ESI and LWF which provide several benefits to your employees.

    Flexible Payment Methods

    You can either automate salary payments through direct bank transfers or you can manually write your employees cheques.

    Track Employee Loans

    Record the loans that you've issued to your employees and collect repayments as a part of each month's pay run.

    Enable Employee Portal

    Enable employees to access their payslips and perform payroll related activities like Income Tax Declaration and Proof of Investment submission through the dedicated employee self-service portal.

    Easy to Switch

    Move your existing payroll data from your current payroll software into Zoho Payroll without duplicate or missing entries, so you can continue processing payroll without interruptions.
