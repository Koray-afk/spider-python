Why should you use Zoho Payments?

There are benefits to choosing a unified payment solution over a third-party solution:

Built natively for your finance apps

Zoho Payments is a part of the Zoho ecosystem. So, you can collect payments right from your finance apps.

No more switching between payments and finance apps

Get payment information and analytic reports right from your finance app, contextually.

You don't need a third-party payment account

Get started right from your Zoho Finance app. As a unified solution, Zoho Payments is automatically linked with your Zoho Finance apps.

One solution to work with

Zoho Payments is a part of your finance apps, so you only have to work with one solution—from onboarding to support.

Unwavering trust

With 25+ years of experience as a reliable software vendor, you can trust us to process your payments safely and securely.
